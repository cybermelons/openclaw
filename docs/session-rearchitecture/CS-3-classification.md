## 7. Pre-existing, out-of-scope test failures found while regression-checking CS-3b

  unrelated to entry-CAS on its face. Also reproduces unmodified on the CS-3a tip commit;
  unrelated subsystem, not investigated further (out of scope for CS-3b).
